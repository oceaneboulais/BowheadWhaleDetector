Dataset coverage: 519,815 unique spectrograms (after deduplication across 5 .dir archives), spanning years 2008, 2010, 2012, 2014 at Sites 3 & 5, DASARs A / D / G. Each spectrogram is a 121×104 uint8 SNR_gram (10–450 Hz, ~8.5-second window).

Coordinates: Mean DASAR positions derived from published maps and S1 Table of Blackwell et al. (2015). Individual DASAR positions within each site are approximated from the published equilateral-triangle grid geometry (7 km side length). Exact GPS positions for all 42 DASAR locations are available in the open-access supplement at doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0125720.s001.
